A routine that leaves room for you

Your next step, delivered.

01 / RECEIVE

Open your daily email.

Each active day brings five questions with prompts, constraints, and examples. Start with foundations and move through a shared coding curriculum at your own mailing pace.

02 / PRACTICE

Work on your own terms.

Solve the questions wherever you like. The mailer keeps your place in the question sequence; it does not grade answers or record which questions you complete.

03 / CONTINUE

There’s a next batch.

AI automatically drafts and reviews new questions when needed. If generation is unavailable, you may receive saved review questions while your place stays unchanged.

You set the pace

A reply is all it takes.

Put a command on the first nonempty line of your reply. Commands are checked at scheduled runs, before the next daily send. Without a command, emails continue each active day.

PAUSE
Take a break and keep your place.
PAUSE 7
Pause for seven days, then resume automatically.
RESUME
Continue with your next batch after a pause.
STOP
Stop daily emails. Contact the organizer to join again.
